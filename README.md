# rnglife
This is a game about life and it is heavily based on rng.
You get the choice of your name and sex and the rest is luck based.
Your income is also luck based and you get 8 options for what to do each day.
You can either Stay home, Work a job, Gamble, Check your status, Play games, Workout, Cry sadly, Feel happy. 
Once you have 0 dollars or helath you die.
